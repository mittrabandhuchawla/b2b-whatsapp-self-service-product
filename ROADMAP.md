
# Product Evolution Roadmap

## Roadmap Context

This roadmap illustrates a proposed progression from structured WhatsApp self-service to AI-assisted B2B conversational commerce.

It represents product planning and dependency-based sequencing, not a historical release schedule or a commitment to delivery dates.

## Stage 1: Existing Self-Service Foundation

**Goal:** Establish reliable customer interactions through structured workflows.

Documented foundation:
- Customer and account identification
- Commercial document requests
- RFQ and inquiry capture
- Complaint registration
- Branch and support information
- Basic new-customer lead capture

**Product learning:** Existing workflows and integrations can serve as reusable building blocks.

## Stage 2: AI-Assisted Product Discovery

**Goal:** Make product discovery less dependent on exact SKU knowledge.

Proposed capabilities:
- Natural-language product interpretation
- Catalog-grounded product matching
- Hindi, English and Hinglish input
- Clarification of incomplete requirements
- Human assistance for unresolved searches

**Dependency:** Structured and sufficiently accurate product data.

## Stage 3: Conversational Order Submission

**Goal:** Enable customers to prepare and submit an order request within WhatsApp.

Proposed capabilities:
- Basket creation and editing
- Approved pricing lookup
- Order summary
- Explicit order confirmation
- Backend order request submission
- Acknowledgement and status handling

**Dependency:** Reliable order processing, customer authorization and commercial controls.

## Stage 4: Experience Optimization

**Goal:** Improve completion rates and reduce avoidable support effort.

Potential enhancements:
- Improved search relevance
- Better clarification prompts
- Reduced conversation friction
- Analysis of abandoned journeys
- Enhanced escalation handling

**Dependency:** Reliable event tracking and customer feedback.

## Prioritization Approach

Prioritize features according to:

1. Customer value
2. Business impact
3. Technical feasibility
4. Data readiness
5. Operational risk
6. Reusability of existing capabilities

## Tradeoffs

### Natural Language vs. Structured Menus

Recommendation: Support both. Natural language improves flexibility while menus provide a useful fallback.

### Reuse vs. Rebuild

Recommendation: Preserve effective existing workflows and replace them only when a clear product or technical benefit exists.

### Automation vs. Human Intervention

Recommendation: Automate routine, well-defined activities and retain escalation for uncertain or commercially sensitive requests.

### Broad Scope vs. Reliable Core Experience

Recommendation: Validate the product discovery and ordering journey before expanding into additional AI features.

---

*This is an illustrative, dependency-based roadmap. No dates, releases or achieved outcomes are implied.*
