
# Success Metrics and Measurement Framework

## Measurement Objective

Evaluate whether conversational self-service improves customer convenience, product discovery, operational efficiency and the quality of business inquiries.

The metrics below are proposed measurements. They are not claims of achieved business results.

## North Star Metric

**Successfully completed eligible customer journeys through WhatsApp**

This captures whether the product helps users complete meaningful tasks rather than merely generating conversation volume.

## Product Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Self-Service Completion Rate | Completed eligible journeys / started eligible journeys | Measure automation effectiveness |
| Product Search Success Rate | Searches resulting in a relevant confirmed product / total searches | Evaluate discovery |
| Order Request Completion Rate | Submitted order requests / initiated eligible order journeys | Measure ordering usability |
| Clarification Rate | Searches requiring clarification / total searches | Identify product-data or interpretation gaps |
| Human Escalation Rate | Escalated eligible interactions / total eligible interactions | Monitor support dependency |
| Repeat Usage Rate | Returning users / eligible users during the measurement period | Measure continued usefulness |

## Operational Metrics

- Average time to complete a routine customer request
- Number of requests requiring manual support
- Failed integration requests
- Unresolved customer interactions
- Order request versus accepted order reconciliation
- Support handoff completion

## Business Metrics

- Qualified business inquiries originating through WhatsApp
- Submitted orders originating through conversational journeys
- Accepted orders resulting from those requests
- Invoiced revenue associated with verified transactions
- Repeat ordering among eligible customers

## Measurement Safeguards

- A conversation is not automatically a qualified lead.
- An inquiry is not an accepted order.
- An order request is not a completed sale.
- Revenue should be attributed to verified commercial transactions.
- Internal test interactions should be excluded from production KPI reporting.
- Actual improvements require baseline and follow-up measurement.

## Evidence Status

At the time of preparing this public case study, verified baseline data, adoption results and financial impact have not been included.

No percentage improvements, revenue gains or cost savings are claimed.

## Product Management Learning

The measurement framework should be defined alongside requirements, not after development.

A useful product is one that improves meaningful customer and business outcomes, not simply one that introduces new technology.
